The finite policy uses natural-number equality tokens, not cryptographic hashes. Authority/rights/clock/validation inputs and authentic receipt issuance are trusted. It is not a proof of implementation refinement, model quality, anonymity, security, system isolation, solver correctness, provider behavior, race freedom, or commercial readiness. The model is intentionally not a formalization of every JSON sentence.
